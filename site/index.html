Twelve styles, and plugins for more

Twelve ways to write a picture.

Each style places letters its own way. Point at a tile, or tap it, to see the photo under it. Stack styles as layers, fade them toward a shape, and turn, mirror or color every letter.

Background

  • Text portrait

    Your text in rows. Each letter shows the photo inside it.

  • Word cloud

    Your words packed inside a shape from the photo, biggest first.

  • Word mosaic

    Large words in calm areas, small ones in the details.

  • ASCII

    Each cell gets the character whose ink matches the tone.

  • Halftone

    A halftone screen where every dot is a letter.

  • Braille

    Fine dots that still copy as plain text.

  • Calligram

    Text poured into a shape: your subject, a word or a heart.

  • Spiral

    One sentence on a spiral or on rings.

  • Flow lines

    Text along the edges and forms, like an engraving.

  • Relief

    Rows of text lifted by the tones, like a terrain map.

  • Typewriter

    Overstruck characters with ribbon ink and a little wobble.

  • Line text

    Your words along drawn or traced lines.

How it works

Every letter finds its place.

  1. Open a photo or start from a blank artboard.
  2. Pick a style, or let Apply text art choose one for the picture.
  3. Export for screen, print, a T-shirt or a README.

A real design studio

Photo, vector and text, in three layers.

Crop, retouch and remove backgrounds on the Photo layer. Draw on the Vector layer. Set the order, opacity and blend of each layer, like in a design app.

  • Text to outlines and centerlines. Letters become paths you can cut, plot or engrave.
  • Groups and Distort. Bulge, pinch, twist, stretch or arc lines and groups around a point.
  • Fills. Solid, linear and radial fills that show the same in every export.
  • Snapping. To ends, corners, centers, crossings, tangents, text baselines, edges in the photo and a grid.
  • Find any command with Ctrl K. Every action has a menu item and a shortcut.

Right to left

Urdu and Arabic, with joined letters.

Arabic script joins its letters, so SungFluence never draws it one letter at a time. Calligram, Text portrait and Spiral set whole words, right to left, in the bundled Noto Nastaliq Urdu and Noto Naskh Arabic fonts.

The name comes from Urdu. Sungam is the place where two rivers meet and flow on as one, a confluence in English. Here, pictures and letters meet.

Plugins and privacy

Plugins

Write your own style.

A plugin is one JavaScript file. It gets the photo's tones and colors, places letters, and every export works with it.

Sungfluence.plugin({
  api: 1, id: 'dots', name: 'Dots',
  *render(api) {
    // an A wherever the photo is dark
    for (let y = 0; y < api.H; y += 12)
      for (let x = 0; x < api.W; x += 12)
        if (api.ink(x, y) > 0.4)
          api.addText('A', x, y);
  }
});

Private by design

Your photos stay with you.

  • Everything happens on your own computer. Photos are never uploaded.
  • No account and no analytics. This website has no trackers either.
  • Works offline. The fonts and help pages are inside the app.
  • Heavy work runs in small steps, so the window never freezes.
