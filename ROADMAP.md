# SungFluence roadmap

SungFluence started as a weekend project. The aim now is a proper design studio for art made of letters: precise, fast, printable, and at home in every script.

These are the directions the project is heading in. They are not promises, and there are no dates.

## A real studio

- A full menu bar and a command palette, so every action is easy to find.
- More layers, which you can duplicate, mask and blend, so effects can be stacked.
- Better vector drawing and editing.
- One project file that keeps everything, and lets you choose what to save.
- No freezing, even on large prints, by moving the heavy work off the main window.
- Help that ships with the app, a user reference and a short tutorial.

## Every language, done properly

- Right-to-left languages, such as Urdu, Arabic and Persian, with their letters joined correctly in every style.
- Top-to-bottom languages, such as Chinese and Japanese, written in columns.
- Your own installed fonts, when you want them.
- New ways to set text that no other tool offers.

## Output for print, offices and makers

- Print-ready PDF with real fonts and color profiles, and simple print setup.
- More formats for everyday office apps and for text output.
- Files for makers: plotters, lasers, stencils and needlework.

## Workflow

- Batch work: one look applied to many photos.
- Recipes you can share with a team.
- Better mockups for printing on clothes.
- Motion: text art that moves.

## Optional AI, your choice

- Optional help from AI services with your own keys, and on-computer tools that need no key at all.
- Nothing leaves your computer unless you say so.

## Plugins

- Plugins that run safely on their own, more kinds of plugins, and a gallery to find them.

## Ideas and help

Have an idea, or want to help with one of these? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/datakeen/Sungfluence/issues). Every release is listed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
