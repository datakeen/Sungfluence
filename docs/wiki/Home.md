Welcome to the SungFluence wiki.

**SungFluence** turns pictures into art made of letters: word portraits, calligrams, ASCII and Braille art, spirals of text, typewriter drawings and more, with Urdu, Arabic and Persian set as properly joined words. It is free, open source and runs on your own computer.

## Start here

- **Download:** [latest release](https://github.com/datakeen/Sungfluence/releases/latest) for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- **Getting started:** a five-minute tutorial, in the app under **?** → **Help and tutorial** (or press F1), and [online](https://github.com/datakeen/Sungfluence/blob/main/docs/help/getting-started.md).
- **All help pages:** [docs/help](https://github.com/datakeen/Sungfluence/blob/main/docs/help/index.md). The same pages ship inside the app, so they work offline.
