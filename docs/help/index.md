# SungFluence Help

SungFluence turns pictures into art made of letters. Everything happens on your own computer; photos are never uploaded.

New here? Start with **[Getting started](getting-started.md)**. It takes five minutes.

## Learn

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): from a photo to a finished piece, step by step.
- [The window](workspace.md): the menus, finding any command with Ctrl K, views, compare and zoom.
- [Writing in Urdu and Arabic](urdu-and-arabic.md): Nastaliq, joined words, right to left.

## Reference

- [Photo panel](photo.md): crop, turn, adjust, effects, remove areas.
- [Vector panel](vector.md): lines, shapes, text, images, step and repeat.
- [Text art panel](text-art.md): recipes, layers, styles and their shared settings.
- [Text art styles](styles.md): every style and every setting, with ranges and starting values.
- [Output and export](output-and-export.md): paper, size, print, every export format, projects.
- [Plugins](plugins.md): extra effects, and how to add more.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md)
- [Problems and answers](troubleshooting.md)

## Get help or give feedback

Found a bug, or have an idea? Please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/datakeen/Sungfluence/issues). Click **New issue**, give it a short title, and describe what happened. A screenshot helps.
