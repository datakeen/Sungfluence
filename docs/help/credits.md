# Open-source credits

SungFluence is free software under the GNU AGPL, version 3 or later. It is built on open-source work by many people. Thank you to all of them.

## What SungFluence includes

- **jsPDF** (MIT) makes the PDF files, with fflate, an Adobe JPEG encoder and other small parts inside it.
- **JSZip** (MIT) makes the ZIP files, with pako, lie, immediate and setImmediate inside it.
- **Electron** (MIT) is the desktop window, with Chromium inside it. The installers are made with **electron-builder** (MIT) and, on Windows, **NSIS**.
- **Typefaces:** Inter, JetBrains Mono, Oswald, Playfair Display, Noto Naskh Arabic and Noto Nastaliq Urdu, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and Special Elite by Astigmatic, under the Apache License 2.0. The font files come from Fontsource.
- Some one-line arts and faces in the Type toolbox are well-known internet kaomoji, also collected in the ART library by Sepand Haghighi, which inspired the toolbox.

## Read every license

Choose **Help → Open-source credits**, or click **?** at the top right, then **About and license**, then **Open-source credits and licenses**. The window lists every project with its copyright, and the full text of each license below. It works offline.

The same notices come with every copy of SungFluence: the file `NOTICE.md` and the `licenses` folder, inside the desktop app, in the web app, and in the source code. On the website they are on the **Credits** page.

The built-in plugins and the plugin template are under the MIT License, so you can borrow from them for your own plugins.
