# Text art panel

The Text art panel decides which letters are drawn, and how.

## Text art uses

**Source** chooses what the letters follow: the **Photo** (its tones), the **Lines** of the Vector layer, or **Both**. Line text needs lines; trace or draw some in the Vector panel first.

## Start from a recipe

Each card is a complete look: style, paper and colors, sometimes two layers together. Click one, then change anything you like. Your own text is kept.

## Layers

Text art can have several layers of its own, drawn one over the other, for example fine Braille under bold words. **+ Add layer** adds one. On each row: the eye shows or hides it, the copy button duplicates it, the arrow moves it up, and the bin deletes it. Click a layer's name to edit it.

## Style

The tiles choose the style of the selected layer. **Extra effects** below them are styles from [plugins](plugins.md). Every style and every setting is listed in [Text art styles](styles.md).

**Variations** shows several takes on the current style. Click one to use it.

## Shared settings

Under each style's own settings are four sections. Click a name to open it.

- **Letters**: weight from thin to bold, letter spacing, italic and capitals.
- **Focus and fade**: fade the letters toward an oval, a circle, a band or a column. The shape shows on the picture while this section is open: drag the dot to move it, the square handles for size, width and softness, and the round handle to turn it (Shift snaps to 15 degrees). **Strong part** keeps the center or the outside strong; **Fades** thins the letters out, makes them see-through, or both.
- **Opacity, effects and inks**: letter opacity; see-through light areas; shadow, outline or riso offset; hand-set wobble; and **Limit to inks**, which reduces the colors to 2 to 8 inks for screen print. Click any ink chip to choose another color for it.
- **More controls**: contrast, brightness and gamma of the tones the style reads; photo color strength; invert; auto levels; draw only on dark parts, light parts, or inside or outside a shape; layer opacity; and the variation number (seed).

## Color

Most styles offer:

- **Ink**: one color. Pick a swatch, any color, a color from the photo, or (in the desktop app) the eyedropper to take a color from anywhere on screen.
- **Photo**: each letter takes the photo's color under it. **Average the color under each letter** gives truer color; **Color strength** makes it bolder; **Brighten dark colors** (or **Deepen pale colors** on light paper) keeps letters visible.
- **Tone**: one ink, lighter or darker with the photo.
- **Duotone**: two inks, one for the shadows and one for the highlights.

## Spiral and Rings on the picture

With a Spiral style selected, the picture shows its center, where the text starts and where it ends, and an arrow for the direction. Drag them. **Text starts** (at the center or outside) and **Direction** (clockwise or anticlockwise) are in the style's settings.
