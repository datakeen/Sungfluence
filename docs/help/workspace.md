# The window

## Menu bar

Every action is in the menus: **File**, **Edit**, **Image**, **Layer**, **Text art**, **Effects**, **Export**, **View** and **Help**. In the desktop app they are the normal menu bar of the window; in a browser they are a bar at the top of the page. Each menu item shows its keyboard shortcut.

## Find any command (Ctrl K)

Press **Ctrl K** (Cmd K on a Mac), or choose **Help → Find a command**, and type a few letters: a command, a style, a recipe, a sample, an export format or a tool. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click. Items that are not available right now are dimmed.

## Top bar

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | A blank artboard. Pick a size in pixels, inches, millimeters or grid cells, or a preset such as Instagram post or A4. The small **+** next to the samples makes a 2000 × 2000 px square right away. |
| **Open** | A photo, or a SungFluence project (`.json`). You can also drag a file onto the window, or paste a picture. |
| Samples | Pictures to try. Click one to load it. |
| **Photo, Mix, Text** | Views. Photo shows the photo and vector layers, Text shows only the text art, Mix shows the text art over a faded photo. They are quick ways to set the layer eyes. |
| Undo, Redo | Ctrl Z and Shift Ctrl Z. |
| **Plugins** | Add, remove or make extra effects. See [Plugins](plugins.md). |
| Gear | Settings: zoom speed and behavior, theme, and gentle mode for slow computers. |
| **?** | Keyboard shortcuts, Help, and About. |
| **Compare** | A divider on the picture with a different look on each side. Drag the divider; choose each side's look in the bar that appears. |
| **Original** | Hold to see the photo for a moment. |
| **Export** | Every way to save your work. See [Output and export](output-and-export.md). |

## Tools, on the left

| Tool | Key | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move around | H | Drag to move when zoomed in. Space does the same with any tool. |
| Zoom | Z | Click to zoom in, Alt-click to zoom out, drag a box to fill the window with it. |
| Crop | C | Drag the corners and sides of the box. Enter applies it. |
| Magic wand | W | Select areas of similar color. Shift adds, Alt subtracts. |
| Lasso | L | Draw around an area to select it. |
| Selection brush | B | Paint a selection. Alt paints it away. |
| Select | V | Move, scale and rotate shapes, text and images. |
| Shapes | U | Rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star and line. |
| Text | T | Click to type. |
| Pen | P | Click for corners, drag for curves. Enter finishes the line. |
| Pencil | N | Draw freely. |
| Edit points | A | Move the points and handles of lines. |

The bar above the picture shows the options of the current tool.

## Layers, on the right

At the top of the right side are the three layers: **Text art**, **Vector** and **Photo**.

- The **eye** shows or hides a layer.
- Drag a layer by its grip to change the order. The top layer is drawn last.
- Each layer's panel starts with its **opacity** and **blend mode**.
- In the Text art panel, **Keep this text art while I edit the photo** locks the letters. Then you can change or cut the photo and put it above the text art, so the letters show through where the photo is clear.

## Panels

Below the layers are four panels: [Photo](photo.md), [Vector](vector.md), [Text art](text-art.md) and [Output](output-and-export.md).

## Bottom bar

- **Zoom**: minus, the zoom size (click it for presets and a slider), plus, and **Fit**.
- **Preview**: Fast, Balanced or Full. A smaller preview keeps the app quick; exports always use the full size.
- The status line shows the preview size, the export size and how many letters are drawn.

## Zoom

Ctrl and the mouse wheel, or a pinch on a trackpad, zoom smoothly around the pointer. Double-click with the Move tool to switch between Fit and 100%. Settings (the gear) change the speed, the glide, the step size and what the wheel does without Ctrl.
