# The window

## Menu bar

Every action is in the menus: **File**, **Edit**, **Object**, **Image**, **Layer**, **Text art**, **Effects**, **Export**, **View** and **Help**. In the desktop app they are the normal menu bar of the window; in a browser they are a bar at the top of the page. Each menu item shows its keyboard shortcut. On a narrow screen, such as a phone, the menus wrap onto a second row. The top bar keeps **New** and **Open** next to the name, with the views, Undo and **Export** on the row below. On a tablet it takes two rows. On a touch screen the hint above the picture lists only what a finger can do.

On the website, during your first visit, the end of the menu bar shows a short note with a **Download** link for the installed app. It never covers the picture, and phones and narrow windows do not show it. Close it with **×**. Later, type *download* in the command palette (Ctrl K) to find **Download SungFluence for your computer**.

## Find any command (Ctrl K)

Press **Ctrl K** (Cmd K on a Mac), or choose **Help → Find a command**, and type a few letters: a command, a style, a recipe, a sample, an export format or a tool. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click. Items that are not available right now are dimmed.

## Top bar

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | A blank artboard. Pick a size in pixels, inches, millimeters or grid cells, or a preset such as Instagram post or A4. The small **+** next to the samples makes a 2000 × 2000 px square right away. |
| **Open** | A photo, or a SungFluence project (`.sungfluence`, or `.json` from older versions). You can also drop a file anywhere on the window, or paste a picture. A pasted picture opens as the new photo; with the Select, Shape or Text tool, or in the Vector panel, it is placed on the artwork as an image instead. |
| Samples | Pictures to try. Click one to load it. |
| **Photo, Mix, Text** | Views. Photo shows the photo and vector layers, Text shows only the text art, Mix shows the text art over a faded photo; click **Mix** again to set how strongly the photo shows. They are quick ways to set the layer eyes. |
| Undo, Redo | Ctrl Z and Shift Ctrl Z. |
| **Plugins** | Add, remove or make extra effects. See [Plugins](plugins.md). |
| **Type toolbox** | Styled letters, symbols, faces, decorations, text ready for sharing and keyword expansions (Shift Ctrl T). Under Text art in the menu, and above every text box. |
| Gear | Settings: zoom speed and behavior, theme, motion (animations, and how text art appears), and gentle mode for slow computers. |
| **?** | Keyboard shortcuts, Help, and About. |
| **Compare** | A divider on the picture with a different look on each side. Drag the divider; choose each side's look in the bar that appears. The divider can run up and down, across, slanted, or at any angle with the slider; Shift and the arrow keys on the divider turn it in 5° steps. |
| **Original** | Hold to see the photo for a moment. |
| **Export** | Every way to save your work. See [Output and export](output-and-export.md). |

## Tools, on the left

| Tool | Key | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move around | H | Drag to move when zoomed in. Space does the same with any tool. |
| Zoom | Z | Click to zoom in, Alt-click to zoom out, drag a box to fill the window with it. |
| Crop | C | Drag the corners and sides of the box. Enter applies it. |
| Magic wand | W | Select areas of similar color. Shift adds, Alt subtracts. |
| Lasso | L | Draw around an area to select it. |
| Selection brush | B | Paint a selection. Alt paints it away. |
| Select | V | Move, scale and rotate shapes, text and images. |
| Shapes | U | Rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star and line. |
| Text | T | Click to type. |
| Pen | P | Click for corners, drag for curves. Enter finishes the line. |
| Pencil | N | Draw freely. |
| Edit points | A | Move the points and handles of lines. |

The bar above the picture shows the options of the current tool.

## Layers, on the right

At the top of the right side are the three layers: **Text art**, **Vector** and **Photo**.

- The **eye** shows or hides a layer.
- Drag a layer by its grip to change the order. The top layer is drawn last.
- **Layer blending**, folded at the end of the Photo and Vector panels, holds the layer's **opacity**, **blend mode** and place in the stack. The Text art panel has them in its **Layer** section.
- In the Text art panel, **Keep this text art while I edit the photo** locks the letters. Then you can change or cut the photo and put it above the text art, so the letters show through where the photo is clear.

## Panels

Below the layers are four panels: [Photo](photo.md), [Vector](vector.md), [Text art](text-art.md) and [Output](output-and-export.md).

## Bottom bar

- **Zoom**: minus, the zoom size (click it for presets and a slider), plus, and **Fit**.
- **Preview**: Fast, Balanced or Full. A smaller preview keeps the app quick; exports always use the full size.
- The status line shows the preview size, the export size and how many letters are drawn.

## Transparency

Where the file stays clear, the artboard shows a checkerboard. **View > Show transparency as** shows it as **Checkerboard**, plain **Black** or plain **White** instead; this changes the screen only, and exported files stay clear. The same choice sits under the Transparent swatch in the Background section and in Settings, and SungFluence remembers it on this computer. The note under the picture, **Transparent background**, says how it is shown. A clear artboard also gets a thin edge, so you can see where it ends. The squares stay about 9 screen pixels at any zoom. When the picture is out of sight, as on a phone where the panel sits below it, a short message says what changed, with a **Show** button that brings the picture back into view.

## Zoom

Ctrl and the mouse wheel, or a pinch on a trackpad, zoom smoothly around the pointer. Double-click with the Move tool to switch between Fit and 100%. Settings (the gear) change the speed, the glide, the step size and what the wheel does without Ctrl.

Past 100%, the part of the picture on screen is drawn again at the resolution of your screen, so the letters stay sharp instead of going blocky. It happens on its own, a moment after the view stops moving, and only for what you can see, so a deep zoom is no slower than a shallow one. While you are still dragging or zooming you see the quick version. Exports never depended on this: the PNG is drawn at the full artboard size, and SVG and PDF are true vector at any size.

## Rulers and guides

**View → Show rulers** (Ctrl R) shows rulers along the top and the left of the picture. They measure the artboard in pixels of the export, with 0 at its top-left corner. The numbers follow you as you zoom and scroll, and a thin line on each ruler shows where the pointer is. The artboard's own stretch of each ruler is a little brighter. The rulers show on the Artboard view only, not in the Paper and T-shirt previews.

The rulers count export pixels, so when you zoom far in, their ticks do not line up with the larger squares of the preview, which is smaller than the export.

To measure in millimeters or inches, right-click a ruler or click the small square where the rulers meet, and pick a unit. The square shows the unit in use: px, mm or in. Millimeters and inches use the printed size from the Output panel. The rulers stay on until you turn them off.

Guides help you line things up. They are thin cyan lines across the picture.

- **Add one**: press on the top ruler and drag down for a horizontal guide, or on the left ruler and drag right for a vertical one. Hold **Alt** to get the other direction. A small tag shows the place, and what the guide snapped to, while you drag.
- **Add one by typing**: **View → New guide…** asks for the direction and the distance from the artboard's edge. It works without a mouse.
- **Move one**: with the Hand, Zoom, Select or Edit points tool, point at a guide until the cursor changes, then drag it. With any other tool, hold **Ctrl** (Cmd on a Mac) first. A shape, a line or a handle on top of a guide comes first, so you can still move and resize a shape that sits on a guide. Hold **Ctrl** to pick up the guide instead.
- **Place it exactly**: double-click a guide and type the distance from the artboard's edge.
- **Delete one**: drag it back onto a ruler. The guide turns into a dashed line and the tag says **Release to delete**. Let go to delete it, or drag back onto the picture to keep it.
- While you drag a guide, it snaps to the edges and the center of the artboard and to the points of lines and shapes. Hold **Alt** to place it freely.

Shapes, lines, the pen and points you edit snap to guides and to the places where guides cross (see Snapping on the Vector panel page). **View** has **New guide…**, **Show guides**, **Lock guides** and **Clear guides**; so does the small square where the rulers meet. Guides are saved with the project and are part of undo. They never appear in exports.

## Motion

Buttons, windows, menus and panels move with short, quiet transitions. When text art appears after you choose a style or a recipe, the letters fly in from outside the picture and land in their places. This only changes the screen for about a second; your artwork and exports are never touched.

In Settings, under **Motion**, choose **Full**, **Calm** (fades without movement) or **Off**, and whether text art should fly in, fade in or appear at once. If your computer is set to reduce motion, SungFluence keeps still whatever you choose.
